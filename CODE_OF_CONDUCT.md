# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, patient, and considerate. Discuss ideas and technical evidence,
not people. Welcome corrections and make space for different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or reject contributions
that violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in the
repository hosting service.
